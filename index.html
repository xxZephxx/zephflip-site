The intelligence

Hermes reads the market so you don't have to.

Every tool a merchant needs to buy low and sell high — watching prices, volume, and your own offers in real time.

Crash & spike alerts

The instant a watched item swings ~10%+, you're pinged — a crash to buy into, or a spike to sell. Toggle with $spikealerts on.

"Sale likely filled"

Put an item on a GE slot and post your PB/PS. When the market crosses your price, ZephFlip tells you it likely went through — no more staring at the screen.

Private live prices

A personal feed for your watchlist and all eight GE slots — instant buy/sell, recent trades, and the numbers that matter, refreshed on your schedule.

Profit leads

A ranked feed of items clearing your tier's threshold — margin, ROI, and hourly volume, sorted so the best flips rise to the top of your market.

GE slot tracking

Manage every account and all eight slots — buying, bought, selling — with buy-limit timers and a coach that flags the slots needing attention.

ZephScore & integrity

A transparent trade score with its reasons shown — never a black box. Community price evidence is checked against the live market before anyone sees it.
