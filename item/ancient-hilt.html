Flipping Ancient hilt on the Grand Exchange

Ancient hilt is a high-value drop used to build the Zaros godsword, which makes it a candidate merchants watch on the RuneScape Grand Exchange. The numbers above are pulled live whenever you load this page: the buy price is roughly what you'll pay to acquire it, the sell price is what you can offload it for, and the margin is what's left after RuneScape's flat 2% Grand Exchange tax.

A healthy flip needs two things at once: a margin wide enough to be worth a slot, and enough 24-hour volume that your buy and sell offers actually fill. A fat margin on an item that trades twice a day is a trap — you'll sit on it. That's why the volume figure matters as much as the spread.

How much can you make flipping Ancient hilt?

Your profit per flip is the margin above multiplied by how many you can move within its buy limit every 4 hours. ZephFlip tracks that limit, the live margin, and the fill likelihood for you — and pings you the moment a resting offer looks filled — so you're not refreshing the GE by hand.
